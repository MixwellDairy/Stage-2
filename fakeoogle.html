CLINICAL TRIAL SUMMARY REPORT
TRIAL ID: APX-0092-PH3 DATE: OCTOBER 14, 2024
COMPOUND: APX-0092 (Neuro-Synaptic Modulator) PHASE: PHASE 2 (HUMAN)
SPONSOR: Apex Bio-Labs Inc. / Sector 4 STATUS: COMPLETED
1. OBJECTIVE & METHODOLOGY
Evaluation of compound APX-0092 administered orally in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials. Primary objective focused on hyper-focused memory retention and cognitive acceleration over a 90-day period. Test Subjects: 120 Candidates (Cohort Alpha & Beta) Dosage Standard: 25mg daily via delayed release capsule.
2. PRIMARY FINDINGS
- 98.4% of subjects demonstrated rapid memory consolidation within 48 hours. - Neural connectivity metrics expanded by an average of 34% across primary pathways. - Mild temporary secondary side effects noted during Phase II were stabilized under current formulation.
3. PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR NOTES
"Phase III trial objectives have been fully met. Compound APX-0092 demonstrates remarkable efficacy. All secondary metrics remain stable under Protocol 7 parameters. Document archived under restricted clearance." Signed, Dr. A. Vance, Lead Clinical Research Specialist Apex Pharmaceuticals - Division of Applied Sciences
